Privacy Policy for Ruby Corporation

Effective Date: July 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our decentralized application (“dApp”) available on the Solana dApp Store. Our dApp, Ruby Corporation, is a platform that requires a Solana wallet login for gameplay and supports external PCK files for minigames played within the app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information, limited to:

  • Blockchain Data: Your public Solana wallet address and transaction history, which are recorded on the Solana blockchain when you log in or interact with the dApp. You have full custody and control of your wallet and private keys.
  • Game Stats: Gameplay data, such as scores, progress, or achievements, associated with your wallet address.
  • Device Information: Automatically collected data, such as IP address, device type, or operating system, to support app functionality and analytics.
  • External PCK Files: Data from minigames loaded via external PCK files, which may include gameplay metrics or user interactions specific to those minigames.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable data. All transactions are processed through a custom smart contract on the Solana blockchain and are not stored in any databases controlled by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Enable login and gameplay through your Solana wallet.
  • Record and display game stats, such as scores or achievements.
  • Support the functionality of external PCK minigames within the app.
  • Improve the dApp’s performance and user experience.
  • Analyze usage trends through aggregated, anonymized data.

Blockchain data, such as your wallet address and transactions, is publicly accessible on the Solana network and not controlled by us.

3. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties that provide hosting, analytics, or technical support for the dApp.
  • Solana Blockchain: Your wallet address and transaction data are publicly recorded on the Solana blockchain via our custom smart contract.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Data from external PCK files may be processed by third-party minigame providers, subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your game stats or device information, where possible, by contacting us at Our Email. Note that blockchain data, such as wallet addresses and transactions processed through our custom smart contract, cannot be modified or deleted due to the immutable nature of the Solana blockchain.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, such as encryption, to protect your game stats and device information. You are solely responsible for securing your Solana wallet and private keys, as we do not have access to or control over your wallet. All transactions are processed securely through our custom smart contract on the Solana blockchain.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

Our dApp may load external PCK files for minigames, which are provided by third parties. These minigames may have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Please review their policies before using those minigames.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data, such as game stats and device information, may be transferred to and stored in jurisdictions outside your country, such as Lithuania. We implement safeguards to protect your data, as required by applicable laws. Blockchain data is stored on the decentralized Solana network, which operates globally.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy and will post the updated version here. The effective date will be updated accordingly.
